PROMPT DE GERAÇÃO
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a completely redesigned high-fidelity desktop web application screenshot for MyCity, a Brazilian civic issue reporting system. The user rejected the previous simplistic generic admin table. The new concept must look like a thoughtful, premium, purpose-built municipal analyst WORKSPACE, mature, distinctive, efficient, ready to implement with React + shadcn. Focus exclusively on the DESKTOP analyst panel, no phone and no presentation board. Full-bleed crisp flat screenshot, landscape approximately 16:10, no browser address bar, no device bezel, no perspective, no external heading or color swatches. All UI copy in accurate Brazilian Portuguese.

Art direction: urban service desk. One bold dark petroleum navigation sidebar, contrasted with a calm light work surface. Structured information hierarchy and precise alignment, not decoration. Palette petroleum #155E75, deep sidebar #103D4A, dark text #0F172A, muted text #526475, pale cool gray canvas #F3F6F8, white #FFFFFF, fine borders #DCE5EA, pale blue selected row #EAF5F8. Small amber blue green status accents only. No gradients, glass, purple, neon, generic hero, charts or decorative dashboard statistics. Typography: distinctive Manrope-like slightly geometric headings and highly legible humanist sans body, 28px main heading, 14-15px readable table/body, confident restrained weights. Numbers clean tabular, not monospace. Moderate corner radii, subtle depth only where it explains layering. Very polished fine line icons, optical alignment, selected state. Design with purposeful density, no vast blank area.

Composition: about 190px left dark sidebar; remaining width is a large operational area. Top compact global bar with breadcrumb 'Gestão urbana / Denúncias', 'Todas as regiões' location selector, a small 'Ambiente de demonstração' badge, profile circle 'GP'. Below heading 'Central de atendimento' and small line 'Analise as ocorrências e acompanhe cada solução.' Then an elegant horizontal status navigation strip integrated into the page, NOT four separate rounded KPI cards: 'Todas 25', 'Enviadas 12', 'Em análise 8' active with petroleum underline, 'Em andamento 5', 'Resolvidas 24' can instead total consistent: use 'Pendentes 25', 'Enviadas 12', 'Em análise 8', 'Em andamento 5', 'Resolvidas 24'. Make selected tab 'Pendentes 25' so list contains mixed pending states. Below this horizontal strip, TWO panes: left 56% width triage queue, right 44% width selected complaint details, separated cleanly by fine vertical border. Keep comfortable padding and sufficiently large readable text. Detail pane isn't a floating modal; it is integrated in the workspace and nearly full-height.

Sidebar: MyCity wordmark with a small precise geometric street/map-pin mark at top, small secondary 'Gestão urbana'. Active navigation 'Atendimento' highlighted by muted lighter petroleum rounded background and small white side indicator, second entry 'Histórico'. Bottom simple profile 'Gestor público' and 'Sair'. Subtle ultra-low-contrast linework evoking an urban street grid only at the bottom of sidebar, confined and barely visible, no map decoration elsewhere.

Left pane: toolbar search input 'Buscar denúncia', filter button 'Filtros' with sliders icon, category chip 'Todas as categorias'. Second small toolbar line '25 denúncias' and sorting 'Mais confirmadas'. A restrained table-like triage list with small column headings 'Ocorrência', 'Status', 'Confirmações'. 6 rows, each about 90px with thumbnail, short bold issue title, small location and age on separate line. Selected first row has pale petroleum background and thin petroleum left indicator; it is '#MC-0248 Buraco na Rua das Flores', 'Centro', 'há 2 horas', blue badge 'Em análise', confirmation number 14 with a small people/check icon. Other rows: 'Iluminação apagada' / 'São José' / 'Enviado' / 11; 'Entulho na calçada' / 'Centro' / 'Em andamento' / 9; 'Coleta não realizada' / 'Santo Antônio' / 'Enviado' / 7; 'Vazamento na via' / 'Centro' / 'Em análise' / 5; 'Árvore com risco de queda' / 'Nova Viçosa' / 'Enviado' / 3. Tiny authentic documentary thumbnails show corresponding Brazilian urban street problems, no people. Bottom pager subdued '1–6 de 25' with previous/next controls. No irrelevant stars, invented urgent priorities, graphs or export buttons.

Right pane: top line '#MC-0248' and blue 'Em análise' badge; heading 'Buraco na Rua das Flores' on up to two lines. Location 'Rua das Flores, Centro' with location icon. Underneath show one strong documentary photo of a pothole on a Brazilian residential street at about 150px height, plus two small photo thumbnail selectors over or just beneath it, within 3 total photos. Compact summary '14 confirmações' alongside small category 'Buraco na via'. Brief citizen description: 'Buraco próximo à esquina, dificultando a passagem de veículos.' Small outlined location strip containing a minimalist authentic-looking street-map thumbnail with one pin, street names, next to 'Localização da ocorrência' and 'Ver no mapa' (this is the location of selected issue, not a new analytics feature). Then a small horizontal divider, label 'Histórico' and two compact timeline entries with connected subtle dots: 'Denúncia enviada' / 'Hoje, 09:42'; 'Em análise' / 'Hoje, 10:15 — Gestor público'. Below compact public-response composer 'Escrever resposta pública...' with separate modest 'Publicar resposta' button. Anchored bottom action area, primary solid petroleum button 'Iniciar atendimento' with arrow icon, secondary text button 'Rejeitar' muted red. Selected complaint is Em análise, so the next valid action is Em andamento; DO NOT show 'Resolver' as primary or skip status. No assignment to teams, deadlines, chat, SLAs or unapproved functionality.

Make it feel noticeably more designed than a template: strong navigation silhouette, excellent typography, quiet dividers instead of card nesting, meaningful evidence photography, persistent selected-case context, carefully balanced dense triage and clear next action. No made-up government URL, no government seal, no marketing embellishment. This is a new conceptual image, not an implemented screenshot.
